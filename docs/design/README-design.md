# Clear Steps

The visual language turns a large task into a small sequence. A few carefully aligned forms carry the idea of progression, while open space gives each decision room to stand on its own. The composition should feel calm at first glance and precise on closer inspection.

Deep charcoal creates a steady foundation. Warm white carries the main message; mint identifies the active step. Muted blue and violet distinguish supporting stages without competing for attention. The palette is deliberately narrow, with painstaking attention to readable contrast.

Typography does most of the work. A large title establishes identity, a short supporting line gives purpose, and small labels explain the sequence. Meticulously crafted spacing and restrained line lengths matter more than ornament.

Three rising cards suggest progress without implying a perfect linear journey. Their connectors remain secondary to the labels. The final execution should show expert care in alignment, edges, and balance, with no animation or decoration that interrupts reading.
